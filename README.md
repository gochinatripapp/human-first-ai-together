# Human First, AI Together
## Building a Future Where Artificial Intelligence and Humanity Prosper Together

**AI should make humanity more capable, not less necessary.**

Welcome to **Human First, AI Together** — an open philosophy initiative exploring how artificial intelligence can improve human life, expand human capabilities, create meaningful work, and contribute to sustainable shared prosperity.

We believe the future of AI should not be defined solely by how much human labor technology can replace.

It should also be defined by how much more humanity can achieve because AI exists.

---

## 🌍 Our Core Philosophy

AI is not simply a tool for reducing costs or automating existing tasks.

It can help people acquire knowledge, develop skills, solve problems, create new services, and participate in opportunities that were previously inaccessible.

We believe technological progress should consider five dimensions:

1. **Human Capability** — Does AI help people become more capable?
2. **Quality of Life** — Does AI make everyday life healthier, easier, safer, or more fulfilling?
3. **Human Participation** — Does AI create opportunities for meaningful human contribution?
4. **Economic Sustainability** — Can businesses, workers, and consumers benefit from the value created?
5. **Shared Prosperity** — Does technological progress create opportunities for broader and more sustainable human prosperity?

Automation is valuable. But automation alone is not a complete definition of progress.

---

## 🔄 The Human–AI Shared Prosperity Loop

We propose a framework for understanding the relationship between AI development and human economic participation.

**AI Capability**

↓

**Expanded Human Capability**

↓

**New Human-Created Value**

↓

**Meaningful Work and Economic Participation**

↓

**Consumer Demand and Market Development**

↓

**Business Growth and Innovation**

↓

**Further Investment in AI and Human Capabilities**

This is a framework for exploration and practical validation, not an assumption that every AI application automatically creates jobs or prosperity.

Our goal is to encourage the design of AI systems that consider the entire cycle.

## 🏡 Real-World Industry Applications

We believe these ideas must be tested in real human environments, not remain abstract discussions.

Two industry projects provide important practical perspectives.

### 1. Suishiping (随食屏) — AI for Better Everyday Living

Eating and cooking are essential, high-frequency activities in human life.

Suishiping explores how AI can help families:

- Eat more healthily.
- Reduce the daily burden of meal planning.
- Understand ingredients and cooking techniques.
- Prepare more delicious meals.
- Enjoy cooking and creative time in the kitchen.

But the vision extends beyond a smart kitchen product.

We are exploring how kitchen appliance companies could combine regular appliance maintenance, household services, and AI-assisted kitchen experiences to establish stronger relationships with existing customers.

This could create new professional opportunities, such as Home Kitchen Lifestyle Consultants, who combine appropriate appliance service skills, customer education, and voluntary product consultation.

The objective is to improve household life while creating additional opportunities for human participation in the economy.

### 2. GoChinaTrip & SSAP — AI That Respects Physical Reality

Traveling in an unfamiliar country involves real-world constraints that cannot always be solved through language generation or abstract reasoning.

GoChinaTrip and the Survival Semantic Action Protocol (SSAP) explore how AI can help travelers make informed decisions, understand physical constraints, prepare for unexpected situations, and preserve their dignity and autonomy.

The underlying principle is simple:

**AI must help people succeed in the real world, not merely produce answers that sound convincing.**

These projects represent different practical applications of a shared philosophy: technology should strengthen human capabilities and improve real human experiences.

## 🧭 Principles for AI Creators and Businesses

We invite developers, entrepreneurs, researchers, and business leaders to consider:

- Are we expanding human capabilities or merely removing human involvement?
- What new services and professional opportunities could AI enable?
- Are people retaining meaningful choices and decision-making authority?
- Who participates in the economic value created by AI?
- Does the technology solve a genuine human problem?
- Can the business model remain sustainable while improving human well-being?

These questions should influence AI product design from the beginning, not be added as an afterthought.
