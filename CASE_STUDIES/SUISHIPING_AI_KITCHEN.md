# Suishiping (随食屏)
## Reimagining the Kitchen: How AI Can Improve Everyday Life and Create New Human Opportunities

**An Industry Case Study of Human-Centered AI and Shared Prosperity**

### 1. The Opportunity: One of Humanity's Most Frequent Daily Activities

Eating and cooking are not occasional activities. They are fundamental, recurring parts of everyday human life.

Every household faces recurring questions:

- What should we eat today?
- How can we prepare healthier meals?
- Which ingredients are suitable for different dishes?
- How can we make cooking easier and more enjoyable?
- How can we reduce the mental burden of planning meals?
- How can we make better use of the kitchen appliances we already own?

These are not merely recipe-search problems.

They involve nutrition, household habits, ingredient knowledge, cooking skills, personal preferences, time, and family well-being.

We believe this makes the kitchen an important real-world environment for human-centered AI innovation.

### 2. The Suishiping Vision

Suishiping (随食屏) explores a new relationship between artificial intelligence, kitchen appliances, food knowledge, and everyday household life.

Its central purpose is:

**Help people eat more healthily, cook with less stress, prepare more delicious food, and enjoy their time in the kitchen.**

The vision includes four dimensions:

**Healthier Eating**

Help households understand ingredients, nutritional considerations, food combinations, and meal planning based on their needs and preferences.

**Easier Cooking**

Reduce the effort involved in deciding what to cook, understanding ingredients, preparing shopping lists, and organizing daily meals.

**Better Cooking Experiences**

Help people discover new dishes, understand cooking techniques, learn from cooking videos, and improve their practical kitchen knowledge.

**More Enjoyable Kitchen Time**

Create a more engaging kitchen environment through cooking videos, audio, entertainment, inspiration, and personalized experiences.

AI should not force everyone to cook in the same way.

A beginner may need step-by-step guidance. An experienced cook may prefer control, experimentation, and creative freedom.

The technology should adapt to people, rather than making people adapt to the technology.

### 3. From Recipe Applications to an AI Kitchen Companion

Traditional digital recipe experiences often focus on displaying recipes.

But everyday cooking involves a much broader set of needs.

Suishiping explores an integrated experience connecting:

- Ingredient recognition and food knowledge.
- Meat cuts and ingredient selection.
- Recipe discovery and cooking inspiration.
- Extraction of ingredients and cooking steps from cooking videos.
- Household meal planning.
- Shopping lists and ingredient preparation.
- Connections with grocery purchasing services.
- Personalized cooking and nutrition assistance.
- Integration with kitchen appliances and their operating environments.

For example, when someone encounters an unfamiliar beef cut at a supermarket, AI could help explain its characteristics, suitable cooking methods, preparation techniques, and possible dishes.

When someone discovers an interesting cooking video, AI could help organize the ingredients and preparation steps into a usable cooking reference.

The objective is not to replace human cooking judgment.

It is to make useful knowledge more accessible and reduce unnecessary effort.

### 4. A New Opportunity for Established Kitchen Appliance Companies

An important part of this concept concerns the existing customer relationships of kitchen appliance manufacturers.

Companies such as Robam and FOTILE have established appliance businesses, product expertise, customer relationships, and after-sales service networks.

These existing resources could provide a foundation for a new AI-enabled household service model.

Consider the existing relationship:

1. A household purchases a range hood, gas stove, or other kitchen appliance.
2. The manufacturer maintains an ongoing relationship with that household.
3. Periodic maintenance and appropriate product inspections provide opportunities to understand the customer's actual kitchen experience.
4. During a service visit, trained personnel can identify practical problems, explain appliance usage, and understand customer needs.
5. If the household wants a better cooking experience, the service professional can introduce relevant optional solutions, including Suishiping.
6. The household decides whether the additional product or service provides sufficient value.

This creates a potential bridge between existing appliance maintenance services and new AI-enabled household experiences.

Importantly, appliance maintenance should remain a genuine service in its own right, not a disguised sales visit.

### 5. Creating New Human Professional Opportunities

This model introduces an important question:

**Can AI help existing industries create new professional roles instead of treating labor reduction as the only path to productivity?**

One potential role is a Home Kitchen Service and Experience Consultant.

This professional could combine several capabilities:

- Appropriate appliance maintenance and service knowledge.
- Household kitchen usage education.
- Understanding of customer cooking needs.
- Demonstration of AI kitchen products.
- Product consultation and after-sales support.
- Collection of voluntary customer feedback.

AI could help these professionals prepare for visits, access product knowledge, explain features, and provide more consistent customer support.

Rather than simply performing a maintenance task, the professional could become a more valuable service partner to the household.

Potential benefits include:

**For Employees**

New skills, broader responsibilities, professional development, and potential additional income.

**For Appliance Manufacturers**

Stronger customer relationships, additional product opportunities, improved service differentiation, and new sources of recurring value.

**For Households**

Better appliance care, more useful kitchen knowledge, and access to tools that can improve everyday cooking.

**For the Wider Economy**

Potential new service activities, training requirements, local employment opportunities, and supporting business ecosystems.

These opportunities must be validated through real customer demand, sustainable compensation, appropriate training, and responsible service design.

They should not be treated as guaranteed employment outcomes.

### 6. A Different Way to Think About AI and Business Growth

Many discussions about AI productivity focus on how many tasks can be automated and how many labor hours can be eliminated.

Suishiping explores another possibility.

What if AI helps businesses create services that did not previously exist?

What if an existing appliance maintenance network becomes a channel for delivering new household value?

What if AI gives service professionals more knowledge and better tools, allowing them to provide services that are more valuable to customers?

What if customers become more engaged with their existing appliances because those appliances are connected to a continuously improving kitchen experience?

This is a different growth model.

It does not reject automation.

It asks businesses to explore both sides of technological progress:

- What unnecessary work can AI reduce?
- What new valuable human work can AI enable?

Both questions deserve attention.

### 7. The Human–AI Economic Cycle

Suishiping provides a practical model for exploring the Human–AI Shared Prosperity Framework.

**AI Knowledge and Capabilities**

↓

**Better Household Cooking Experiences**

↓

**New Consumer Value and Demand**

↓

**New Products and Services**

↓

**New Human Professional Roles**

↓

**Additional Economic Participation**

↓

**Stronger Customer Relationships and Business Development**

↓

**Further Investment in AI, Products, and Services**

This cycle is a proposed framework for industry experimentation, not a guarantee that every link will automatically occur.

Its success depends on whether households genuinely benefit and whether businesses can create economically sustainable services.

### 8. Important Design Principles

To make this model genuinely human-centered, several principles matter.

**Customer Choice**

Maintenance must not be conditional on purchasing additional products. Product demonstrations and sales should be transparent and voluntary.

**Professional Competence**

Maintenance personnel must receive appropriate technical training. AI assistance must never replace required safety qualifications or professional judgment.

**Real Customer Value**

Products should solve genuine household problems rather than create artificial demand.

**Responsible AI**

Health and nutrition information should distinguish general guidance from individualized medical advice. Uncertainty and limitations should be communicated.

**Data Privacy**

Household preferences, dietary information, and customer records should be collected and used transparently, with appropriate consent and protection.

**Sustainable Employment**

New roles should provide fair compensation, clear responsibilities, appropriate training, and realistic career development.

### 9. An Invitation to the Kitchen Appliance Industry

We invite kitchen appliance manufacturers, AI developers, food technology companies, service providers, and entrepreneurs to explore this opportunity.

The kitchen is not merely a place where appliances operate.

It is a place where people care for families, develop skills, express creativity, make daily decisions, and create meaningful experiences.

AI has the potential to make this part of human life better.

But the opportunity is larger than building another smart appliance or another recipe application.

It is about creating an integrated ecosystem in which technology, products, services, and human professionals work together.

We believe the next generation of kitchen innovation should consider not only smarter machines, but also better household experiences and new opportunities for people.
